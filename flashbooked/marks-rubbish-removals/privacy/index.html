Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Marks Rubbish Removals ("we," "us") provides rubbish and waste removal services in the Dublin area. This policy explains what information we collect when you enquire about a job — including through our Facebook and Instagram lead ads — how we use it, and the third-party tools involved. We do not sell your personal data.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, phone number, email, and property/collection address when you submit a lead form, request a quote, or book a collection.
  • Job details — the type and rough quantity of items or waste you want removed, and any photos or notes you provide to help us quote accurately.
  • Meta Lead Ads data — if you submit one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares the information you entered (name, phone, email) directly with us so we can follow up about your quote.
  • Booking data — when you book a collection slot, that request is handled by our scheduling and CRM provider, GoHighLevel, which processes your name, email, phone, and selected time.

Third-party tools we use

We use the following services to run our ads and manage enquiries. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your quote, confirm job details, and schedule a collection.
  • To send SMS and email updates about your enquiry or booking — by submitting your phone number, you consent to receive these messages; reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • To measure and improve the performance of our own ad campaigns.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Data collected through the tools above is used only for the purposes listed.

How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry and job data for as long as needed to complete your job and handle any follow-up, and no longer than necessary for our business and legal record-keeping requirements.

Your rights under GDPR

As we operate in Ireland, your data is processed under the EU General Data Protection Regulation. You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Your choices

You can decline cookies via your browser settings, which may limit tracking tools from functioning. You can opt out of SMS follow-up at any time by replying STOP. To exercise any of the rights above, contact us below.
