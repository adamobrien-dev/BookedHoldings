Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FlashBooked ("we," "us") provides an AI receptionist service for home service businesses in Ireland. This policy explains what information we collect through flashbooked.com, how we use it, and the third-party tools involved. We do not sell your personal data.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone number, and business details when you book a call, fill out a form, or message us.
  • Booking data — when you use the calendar embed to book a call, that request is handled by our scheduling provider, GoHighLevel, which processes your name, email, phone, and selected time.
  • Usage data — pages visited, referral source, device/browser type, and general location, collected automatically via the Meta Pixel described below.

Third-party tools used on this site

We use the following services to operate, measure, and advertise this site. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule calls.
  • To send SMS and email follow-up about your enquiry or booking — by submitting your phone number, you consent to receive these messages; reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • To measure and improve our own ad campaign performance.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Data collected through the tools above is used only for the purposes listed.

Your rights under GDPR

As we operate in Ireland, your data is processed under the EU General Data Protection Regulation. You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Your choices

You can decline cookies via your browser settings, which may limit the Meta Pixel from functioning. You can opt out of SMS follow-up at any time by replying STOP. To exercise any of the rights above, contact us below.
