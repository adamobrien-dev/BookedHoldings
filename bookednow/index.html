The problem

Ads are working, but bookings are leaking

You are paying for clicks. Too many never become a real appointment on your calendar.

  • After-hours traffic hits a “call us” wall and moves on
  • Third-party booking apps put another brand in the middle of your patient journey
  • Embedded widgets often make it harder to see which ad actually drove the booking
  • No-shows quietly inflate the true cost of every new patient you buy with ads
The fix

One first-party path from ad click to appointment

Send traffic to pages you own. Let patients book in a flow that matches how you really schedule. Keep reporting tied to booked visits—not just clicks.

1

Run ads to your pages

Point Meta and Google campaigns at landing pages and booking links on your domain—not a separate app patients have to figure out.

2

Patients book on their phone

They pick a service, time, and location. Optional deposits cut no-shows. Reminders bring them back when life gets busy.

3

You see what worked

Reporting connects campaigns and pages to booked appointments and revenue—so you can scale what actually fills the schedule.
