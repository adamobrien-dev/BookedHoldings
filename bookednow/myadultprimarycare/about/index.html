✦ About My Adult Primary Care

Personalized Care for a Healthier, Pain-Free Life

My Adult Primary Care is a primary care practice in Fayetteville, TN, offering advanced StemWave regenerative therapy alongside comprehensive primary care. We're focused on one thing: helping our patients feel and function better.

Our Story

My Adult Primary Care was founded to give patients in Fayetteville and the surrounding area access to both everyday primary care and advanced regenerative treatments like StemWave therapy — without having to travel to a specialty clinic.

We take time to understand each patient's health history, pain points, and goals before recommending a plan, whether that's a routine wellness exam or a multi-session StemWave treatment course.

Our Mission

We believe chronic pain shouldn't be something patients simply learn to live with. Our mission is to combine non-invasive regenerative therapy with attentive primary care so patients can address the root of their pain, improve their overall health, and get back to the activities they enjoy.
