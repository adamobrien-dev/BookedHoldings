Comprehensive Telepsychiatry for Adults

Every care plan at Ample Grace Psychiatry starts with a thorough clinical evaluation. We treat the full picture — not just the symptoms.

Adult ADHD Evaluation & Treatment

Our most-requested service. If you've struggled with focus, concentration, executive functioning, or have suspected ADHD for years without a formal evaluation, this is where we start. Our comprehensive adult ADHD evaluation includes a detailed clinical interview, history review, and individualized treatment recommendations.

We see adults 18 and older for ADHD evaluations. Medication management follow-ups are available for patients 9 and older. Initial evaluation: $200. Ongoing medication management visits every 1–3 months.

Women's Mental Health

Women face unique mental health challenges across all life stages — from hormonal shifts and reproductive transitions to caregiving stress and burnout. Our providers offer specialized, compassionate care that takes these factors into account.

We treat anxiety, depression, mood disorders, PMDD, postpartum mood concerns, stress, trauma, and life transitions. Ages 18 and older.

Anxiety & Depression Treatment

Evidence-based, personalized treatment for anxiety, depression, mood disorders, trauma/PTSD, sleep disturbances, and difficulties with emotional regulation. We focus on root causes and sustainable plans — not just symptom management.

Many patients notice initial improvements within 2–4 weeks, with more substantial benefits within 1–3 months depending on their condition and treatment plan.
