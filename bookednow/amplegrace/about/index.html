Psychiatric Care Built Around You

Ample Grace Psychiatry is a telehealth psychiatric practice dedicated to providing compassionate, evidence-based care for adults — without the waitlists, the commutes, or the one-size-fits-all treatment plans.

Who We Are

Ample Grace Psychiatry is a licensed telepsychiatry practice serving adults in Minnesota, Oregon, and Nebraska. We specialize in ADHD evaluations and treatment, women's mental health, and anxiety and depression care — delivered entirely through secure telehealth.

Our providers believe that good psychiatric care starts with listening. Before recommending any treatment, we take the time to understand your full picture: your history, your goals, and the specific challenges that brought you to us.

We treat a range of conditions including ADHD, anxiety, depression, mood disorders, stress and burnout, trauma/PTSD, sleep disturbances, and difficulties with focus and emotional regulation.

Our Approach

We believe that improved focus, reduced anxiety and depression symptoms, better emotional well-being, and an improved quality of life are achievable goals — not distant aspirations. Many patients begin to notice improvements within 2–4 weeks, with more substantial benefits occurring within 1–3 months depending on their condition and treatment plan.

We are an outpatient telehealth practice. We do not provide crisis or emergency psychiatric services, and we do not routinely accept patients whose primary needs include active substance use disorders, severe psychotic disorders requiring intensive management, or conditions requiring inpatient care. If you are unsure whether we are the right fit, please call us — we're happy to help you find the right level of care.
