Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Ample Grace Psychiatry ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website and booking platform located at this domain. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you interact with us, including when you opt in to receive SMS text messages or emails regarding your care.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service, which govern your use of our platform and SMS messaging program.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, book an appointment, or submit an opt-in form, we may collect:

  • First and last name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Date, time, and IP address of consent submission
  • General health and scheduling information you voluntarily provide

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Send you SMS text messages and emails about appointments, care updates, and follow-up communications you consented to receive
  • Contact you to schedule or confirm your appointment
  • Verify insurance and assist with prior authorizations where applicable
  • Maintain a record of your consent as required by applicable law
  • Improve our services and communications

3. SMS / Text Message Communications

By providing your mobile phone number and consenting to receive communications, you expressly consent to receive autodialed and/or manually dialed SMS text messages from Ample Grace Psychiatry at the phone number you provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message. Reply HELP for help. Supported carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in delivering communications (e.g., CRM and SMS platforms) under appropriate data processing agreements
  • Insurance carriers or billing entities, with your authorization, to facilitate care
  • Law enforcement or regulatory bodies when required by law

5. Healthcare Privacy

Ample Grace Psychiatry is committed to protecting your health information in accordance with applicable federal and state laws, including HIPAA where applicable. Clinical information and protected health information (PHI) are handled under separate Notice of Privacy Practices provided at the time of care initiation.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information, including consent records, for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law — typically a minimum of 4 years for SMS consent records.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@amplegracepsych.com.

8. Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
