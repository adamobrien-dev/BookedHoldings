✦ About Advanced Joints Chiropractic

Care Focused on the Root Cause, Not Just the Symptom

Advanced Joints Chiropractic, LLC is a chiropractic and neuropathy care practice in Crowley, TX, led by Dr. Can Ho — dedicated to helping patients find lasting relief through non-invasive, personalized care.

Our Story

Advanced Joints Chiropractic was founded around a simple idea: nerve and joint pain deserve more than a generic visit that only masks symptoms. Every patient who walks through our doors receives a thorough assessment of their symptoms, medical history, and recovery goals before we design a treatment plan.

Based in Crowley, TX, Dr. Can Ho specializes in treating all types of neuropathy — including diabetic neuropathy, chemo-induced neuropathy, and idiopathic neuropathy — alongside chiropractic care for joint and spinal health.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver personalized, patient-focused care that supports the body's natural healing process. We believe in a holistic, evidence-based approach that prioritizes safety, comfort, and long-term results — non-surgical, drug-free, and administered by experienced professionals who listen.
