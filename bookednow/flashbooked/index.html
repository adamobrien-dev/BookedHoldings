Aoife knows her limits

She's a booking system, not a technician — she never improvises safety advice or quotes a price she isn't authorised to give.

  • Suspected gas leak → immediate escalation, no troubleshooting
  • Carbon monoxide alarm, flooding, fire, electrical hazard → immediate human handoff
  • Pricing → only what you've explicitly approved, or "a technician will confirm"

You set the escalation rules

Every business chooses its own handoff logic:

  • Emergency → call the owner directly

  • Aoife uncertain → human escalation, every time

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your existing business number. Calls route to FlashBooked exactly how you choose — after a set number of rings, when you're busy, after hours, or immediately.

What exactly counts as a "qualified booking" for the guarantee?

An appointment booked by FlashBooked from an eligible inbound customer who is within your service area and requesting a service you've authorised FlashBooked to book. Spam, suppliers, job applicants, wrong numbers, out-of-area calls and calls for services you don't offer don't count either way.

What calendar or software does this work with?

Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar on day one, or FlashBooked's own calendar if you don't have one. Other dispatch software can be discussed as a custom integration.

What happens with cancellations or rescheduling?

Customers can call or text Aoife directly to reschedule or cancel — she identifies the booking, updates your calendar, and notifies you. Confirmations and reminders are sent automatically ahead of every appointment.

Is there a limit on call minutes?

Your plan includes a set amount of AI call minutes each month. If you go over, you're notified before any extra charge — no surprise bills.

What can't the AI do?

It never gives technical safety advice, never improvises a price you haven't approved, and always escalates suspected gas leaks, carbon monoxide alarms, flooding, fire, or electrical hazards straight to a human.
