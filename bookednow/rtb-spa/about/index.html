✦ About RTB Spa

Care, Craft, and a Commitment to How You Feel

RTB Spa is a licensed therapeutic massage and wellness studio in Houston, TX, operated by SMS Clear Concepts LLC. We're a small, dedicated team focused on one thing: helping our clients feel better, in their bodies and in their lives.

Our Story

RTB Spa was founded with a simple idea: that quality therapeutic massage shouldn't feel like a luxury reserved for special occasions — it should be a regular, accessible part of taking care of yourself.

Based in the Energy Corridor area of Houston, we've built a practice around certified massage therapists who take time to understand each client's body, lifestyle, and goals before recommending a treatment plan. No two sessions are exactly alike, because no two clients are exactly alike.

Our Mission

We believe consistent, personalized bodywork supports better sleep, lower stress, fewer aches, and a healthier relationship with your own body. Our mission is to make that kind of care approachable — through flexible scheduling, clear communication, and treatments built around you rather than a one-size-fits-all menu.
