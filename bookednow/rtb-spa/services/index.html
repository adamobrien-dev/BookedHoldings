Treatment Menu

💆

Swedish & Relaxation Massage

Long, flowing strokes and gentle kneading designed to reduce stress, improve circulation, and promote overall relaxation. Ideal for first-time clients or anyone needing to unwind.

60 / 90 min sessions available
🏋️

Deep Tissue & Sports Massage

Firm, targeted pressure that reaches deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue. Great for chronic tension, muscle soreness, and supporting athletic recovery and mobility.

60 / 90 min sessions available
🤰

Prenatal Massage

Specialized positioning and gentle techniques to ease pregnancy-related discomfort, reduce swelling, and support relaxation for expecting mothers. Performed by therapists trained in prenatal care.

60 min sessions
🔥

Cupping Therapy

A traditional technique using suction cups to improve blood flow, release muscle tightness, and support recovery. Often paired with massage for enhanced results.

Add-on or standalone

What to Expect

Your first visit starts with a brief conversation about your goals, any areas of tension or pain, and relevant health history. From there, your therapist will recommend a modality and pressure level suited to you — and adjust throughout the session based on your feedback.

We recommend arriving a few minutes early to settle in. Comfortable, loose clothing is fine for most services, and towels/linens are provided for all treatments.
