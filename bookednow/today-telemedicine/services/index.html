What to Expect at Your First Visit

Your first appointment is a conversation — not a quiz. A licensed provider will take time to understand your mental health history, current symptoms, and what you're hoping to get out of care. From there, we'll recommend a treatment path and walk you through every step.

All you need is a phone or computer with a working camera and microphone. We serve women across all of Florida, 100% virtually — no travel required.
