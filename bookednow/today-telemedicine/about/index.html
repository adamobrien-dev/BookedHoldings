✦ About Today Telemedicine

Compassionate Psychiatric Care, Built for Women

Today Telemedicine PLLC is a Florida-licensed virtual psychiatric practice dedicated to helping women access the mental health care they deserve — on their schedule, from anywhere in Florida.

Our Story

Today Telemedicine was founded with a simple belief: women shouldn't have to choose between their mental health and their busy lives. Getting the right psychiatric care shouldn't require taking time off work, arranging childcare, or sitting in a waiting room.

We built a 100% virtual practice so that women across Florida can connect with licensed psychiatric providers from home, work, or wherever they feel most comfortable.

Our Mission

We're here to make quality psychiatric care accessible, confidential, and genuinely convenient. Our providers take the time to understand your full picture — your history, your symptoms, your life — before recommending a treatment plan. No rushed appointments, no cookie-cutter solutions.
