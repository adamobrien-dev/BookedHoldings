✦ About Dr. Ngalame

Collaborative & Holistic Care, Board-Certified

Dr. Jackline Ngalame is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner and Family Nurse Practitioner, with certifications in General, Adult, Child, and Adolescent Psychiatry.

Our Story

Since 2015, Dr. Ngalame has been committed to providing compassionate psychiatric care through both in-person and virtual settings. Before becoming a psychiatric nurse practitioner, she gained extensive experience working with patients of all ages in inpatient psychiatric settings — an experience that deepened her dedication to mental health care.

She now provides telepsychiatry services across Colorado, Florida, Kansas, Minnesota, Nevada, New York, and Washington State, offering evidence-based online mental health care to support clients wherever they are.

Our Approach

Dr. Ngalame's approach is collaborative, holistic, and evidence-based. She works closely with patients, their families, and other health professionals to create individualized, person-centered treatment plans — combining medication management, psychotherapy, and supportive care to help each person achieve lasting emotional wellness.
