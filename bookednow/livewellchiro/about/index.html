✦ About Live Well Chiropractic

Care Focused on How You Move and Feel

Live Well Chiropractic is a chiropractic practice in Spanish Fort, AL. Dr. Swigert and the team are focused on one thing: helping patients live with less pain and better mobility.

Our Story

Live Well Chiropractic was built around a simple idea: chiropractic care should be personal, not rushed. Every patient's pain and history is different, so every care plan should be too.

Based in Spanish Fort, AL, Dr. Swigert works with patients to understand the root cause of their pain before recommending a treatment plan — not just adjusting the symptom in front of them.

Our Mission

We believe consistent, personalized chiropractic care supports better movement, fewer aches, and a healthier relationship with your own body. Our mission is to make that kind of care approachable — through clear communication, honest recommendations, and plans built around you.

What We Value

  • ✓Honest conversations about what your body actually needs
  • ✓A calm, welcoming office environment
