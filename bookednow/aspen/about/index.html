Our Story

Aspen Behavioral Health Services is a psychiatric behavioral health clinic based in Pearland, TX, providing care to patients across the state. Irikefe Ojevwe, a psychiatric nurse practitioner, founded the practice to make quality psychiatric care more accessible — whether that means an in-person visit at the Pearland office or a telehealth appointment from anywhere in Texas.

Every new patient begins with a real conversation about what they're experiencing, so the plan that follows actually fits their situation.

Our Approach

ADHD is the condition seen most often at Aspen Behavioral Health Services — patients complete a comprehensive ADHD questionnaire and evaluation, and when a diagnosis is confirmed, treatment options including medication management are discussed together.
