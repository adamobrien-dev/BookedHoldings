Our Story

Paradise Healing LLC was founded to give clients in Randolph and the surrounding area access to proven holistic modalities — Reiki, energy healing, chakra balancing, and guided meditation — in a warm, supportive setting.

We take time to understand what each client is experiencing before recommending a plan, whether that's a single session or an ongoing healing journey.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe, supportive environment where clients can address stress, tension, and imbalance through proven holistic modalities — without judgment and without pressure. We believe wellness is a journey, and every client's path looks different.
