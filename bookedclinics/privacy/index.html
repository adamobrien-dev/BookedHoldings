Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

BookedClinics ("we," "us") provides digital marketing services for clinics. This policy explains what information we collect through bookedclinics.ca, how we use it, and the third-party tools involved. We do not sell your personal data.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone number, and clinic details when you book a call, fill out a form, or message us.
  • Booking data — when you use the calendar embed to book a call, that request is handled by our scheduling provider, GoHighLevel, which processes your name, email, phone, and selected time.
  • Usage data — pages visited, referral source, device/browser type, and general location, collected automatically via the tools below.
  • Session recordings — mouse movement, clicks, and on-page interactions, collected via Contentsquare (see below) to help us understand and improve the site.

Third-party tools used on this site

We use the following services to operate, measure, and advertise this site. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule calls.
  • To send SMS and email follow-up about your enquiry or booking — by submitting your phone number, you consent to receive these messages; reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • To measure and improve ad campaign performance (our own ads, not on behalf of unrelated third parties).
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Data collected through the tools above is used only for the purposes listed.

Your choices

You can decline cookies via your browser settings, which may limit analytics and session-recording tools from functioning. You can opt out of SMS follow-up at any time by replying STOP. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact us below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data — email hello@bookedclinics.ca.
