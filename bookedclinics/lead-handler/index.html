The Golden Rules

Two Rules That 4X Your Contact Rate

According to a Harvard Business study and data from over 4,000 medical clinics, following these two rules makes you 4× more likely to reach your leads.

1

Speed to Lead

The fastest business to contact the lead first, wins — every single time.

  • Call the lead within 5 minutes of them reaching out. Don't ask, just do it.
  • Double-dial: Call once, hang up, then call again immediately. If they're real, they'll pick up.
2

Persistence

Most leads are lost because of zero follow-up. They reached out to you — you have an obligation to reach back.

  • Call (double-dial) AND text — twice per day, morning and afternoon.
  • Repeat for 3 consecutive days before moving on.
  • If you're too soft, the next practice wins their business.

The Proven Phone Script

A 7-Step Script That Books Appointments

Every line of this script is tested across thousands of clinic calls. Follow it in order and adapt the name of your clinic and promotion.

Step 1
Introduction
Hi, is this Sarah?
Hi Sarah, this is Jessica from ABC Clinic.
I saw you requested information about our [AD PROMOTION] earlier today.
We typically reach out right away just to ask a few questions to make sure we're the right fit AND confirm you're a real person off the internet (haha).
What specifically about our [AD PROMOTION] peaked your interest?
Step 2
Discovery
What is the biggest challenge or goal that has you looking for help?
How long has this been bothering you?
Have you tried anything else to solve this problem or achieve this goal?
How is this affecting your day-to-day life?
What happened recently that made you decide it's time to do something about it?
Step 3
Clarify
So if I'm hearing you correctly…
You've been dealing with this for ______.
It's affecting your ______.
And you're ready to finally do something about it and achieve ______.
Does that sound accurate?
Step 4
Paint the Future
Amazing. So if we were able to [solve their challenge / achieve their goal], how would that impact or change things for you?
Step 5
Book the Appointment
Based on what you've told me Sarah, you sound exactly like the type of person we help here at ABC Clinic.
[Optional] Patient Mary was experiencing the exact same challenge, and we were able to achieve [XYZ result] in [XYZ timeframe].
I have Tuesday at 2:00 PM or Wednesday at 10:00 AM available. Which works better for you?

Always give two specific options — never ask "when are you free?"

Step 6
Accountability Close
Amazing — I have you booked with Doctor John for Wednesday at 10 AM.
Doctor John is very busy and will be setting aside 30 minutes specifically for you. Is there any reason we shouldn't expect you to show up?
Great — we are looking forward to seeing you then!
Step 7
Credit Card (Optional)
To avoid no-shows and out of respect for each other's time, we do require a card on file to reserve the timeslot with the doctor.
It's a $50 no-show fee in the event you totally ghost us (which I know you won't — LOL).
Just give us 24 hours notice if anything changes so we can fill the spot with someone else.
I'm ready for the card when you are!
Objection Handling

The L.A.R.C. Framework

Every objection follows the same four-step framework. Master this and no lead catches you off guard.

1. ListenDon't interrupt
2. AskDig deeper
3. RespondAddress the real concern
4. CloseOffer the time slot

How much does it cost?

Ask Is cost the most important thing, or getting the result?
Respond The investment varies — typically X to Y, but we have payment plans as low as Z/month. Most patients say they'd have paid double.
Close Does tomorrow at 4 PM work for you?

Is it covered by insurance?

Ask Is insurance the most important thing, or getting the result?
Respond Unfortunately not, but our payment plans are often equal to or less than insurance co-pays — and you get results much faster.
Close Does tomorrow at 4 PM work?

It's too expensive.

Ask What exactly are you comparing this to?
Respond If you don't address this now, it typically gets 2–3× more expensive down the road. Most patients don't even think about what they paid once they see results.
Close Does tomorrow at 4 PM work?

I need to check my schedule.

Ask Do you have your phone or calendar on you right now?
Respond I'll hang here and grab a quick water while you grab that.
Close What time works best — Tuesday or Wednesday?

I need to think about it.

Ask Out of curiosity, what exactly do you need to think about?
Respond I've found it's best to answer questions together now — because life gets busy and we delay what's most important. On a scale of 1–10, how do you feel about this?
Close Great — does Tuesday or Wednesday work best?

I need to talk to my spouse.

Ask Does your spouse know about this problem? Would they be opposed to a solution?
Respond Good news — this is just a consultation, no commitment. You can make the final decision together when you come in.
Close Let's get a date booked — worst case you can call me back to adjust. Tuesday or Wednesday?
🤓

Cheat Code: Patient Testimonial Stories

Use real patient stories to relate to the lead's specific objection. "Sarah had the exact same concern, and we were able to [result] in [timeframe]." It builds trust and lowers resistance faster than any other technique.
